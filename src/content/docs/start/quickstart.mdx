You need a Solana wallet, some USDC to spend, and a little SOL for network fees.

<Steps>

1. **Get a Solana wallet.** Any wallet that supports the Solana Wallet Standard works, such as Phantom, Solflare or Backpack. If the app can't find a wallet, its **Get wallet** button links to Phantom.

2. **Fund it.** Add the USDC you want to put into a basket, plus about 0.02 SOL. The SOL pays network fees and small refundable account deposits.

3. **Open the app** at [app.basketsolana.xyz](https://app.basketsolana.xyz) and click **Get wallet** in the top right to connect.

4. **Pick a basket.** The **Indexes** page lists every live basket with its NAV, 24-hour change and TVL. Open one to see what it holds. [Live baskets](/baskets/live-baskets/) describes each one.

5. **Enter a USDC budget** in the **Mint** panel. The app shows how many basket tokens you'll receive, the estimated spend including fees, and how much of your budget goes unused. Unused USDC stays in your wallet.

6. **Click Mint and approve each transaction.** Minting buys every token in the basket, so it takes several transactions, usually three to five. Approve them in order as your wallet asks. When the last one confirms, the basket tokens are in your wallet.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="If something interrupts you">
You don't lose anything if you close the tab or reject a transaction partway through. Reopen the basket's page and the app offers to **continue** where you left off. After 20 minutes, it offers to **recover** the tokens bought so far. See [Finish or recover a mint or redeem](/guides/recover/).
</Aside>

## Redeem later

Switch the panel to **Redeem**, enter how many basket tokens to burn, and approve the transactions. You receive USDC for your share of each token. See [Redeem a basket](/guides/redeem/).

## Earn from fees

If you hold BASKET, stake it on the **Stake** page to earn a share of every mint and redeem fee in USDC. There is no lockup. See [Stake BASKET](/guides/staking/).
