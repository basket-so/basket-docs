title: Create a basket

---

Anyone can create a basket on the app's [Create page](https://app.basketsolana.xyz/create). It's listed in the app under **Community**. Basket's own baskets are marked **Core**.

## Steps

1. **Add tokens.** Up to 40, each at least 0.5%.
2. **Pick a type.** [Fixed weights or fixed units](/baskets/basket-types/).
3. **Set the fee.** From 0.10% to 1%.
4. **Name it.** Pick a symbol, and add a description and logo if you like. These can't be changed later.
5. **Create it.** Approve twice in your wallet.

The basket starts at $1.00 and appears in the app within a few minutes.

## Cost

- 0.1 SOL creation fee
- A few hundredths of a SOL for accounts
- Network fees

## What you earn

10% of every mint and redeem fee, paid in USDC. The rest goes to the Basket treasury (40%) and BASKET stakers (50%).

## Rules

- Basket's keeper does the rebalancing.
- You can't pause the basket or cap its supply.
- Raising the fee takes three days' notice.
- Tokens with transfer fees or a transfer hook are refused. Tokens an issuer can freeze, like tokenized stocks, are allowed.

Full details are in [Security and trust](/protocol/security/#community-baskets). Basket doesn't review community baskets; see [Risks](/resources/risks/#community-baskets).
