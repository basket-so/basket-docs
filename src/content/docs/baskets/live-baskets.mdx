There are {data.baskets.length} baskets live today. Each one is a separate token with its own vaults. For **fixed-weight** baskets the percentages below are targets that rebalancing returns to. For **fixed-unit** baskets they're the weights at launch. The token quantities never change, so the weights move with prices. The app shows each basket's current weights.

Fixed-weight baskets also keep a small USDC slot that rebalancing routes through. It's left out below unless USDC is one of the basket's targets.
